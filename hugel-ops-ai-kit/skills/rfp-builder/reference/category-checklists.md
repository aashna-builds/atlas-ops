# Category prompts

Use as questions to ask the user and requirements to propose — not as settled Hugel requirements.

## 3PL / warehousing / distribution
- Temperature-controlled storage ranges and mapping/validation; excursion handling and monitoring
- Licensing and quality-system expectations for pharma/biologic handling [TO CONFIRM with Quality/Regulatory]
- Lot/batch and expiry tracking, FEFO, recall and quarantine capability
- Order-to-ship SLAs, accuracy, cut-off times, returns/damaged goods
- WMS capability and integration with Hugel's ERP/CRM; EDI/API
- Capacity, peak handling, disaster recovery, inventory insurance and shrink

## Carriers / cold chain transport
- Validated packaging and lanes, transit-time guarantees, real-time temperature tracking
- Chain-of-custody, proof of delivery, claims process and liability limits
- Coverage to clinics and practices; weekend/holiday service

## Systems integrators (Salesforce / SAP / similar)
- Relevant life sciences or commercial-ops integrations; named team and references
- Methodology, timeline, change management and training
- Data migration, security, access control, validation approach
- Support model after go-live; fixed-fee vs. T&M; change-order process

## General
- Exit/transition assistance, termination rights, subcontracting, insurance, KPIs and reporting cadence
