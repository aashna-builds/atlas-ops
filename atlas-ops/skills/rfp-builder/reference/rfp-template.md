# RFP Template (Hugel)

Fill every section; use `[TO CONFIRM: …]` for unknowns.

## 1. Introduction
- About Hugel America (Newport Beach, CA; U.S. arm of Hugel Inc., a medical aesthetics company) — one short paragraph, non-confidential.
- RFP title, reference number, issue date.

## 2. Purpose and Scope
- What Hugel is seeking, the business objective, in-scope / out-of-scope, geography, contract term sought.

## 3. Requirements
Numbered table: ID | Requirement | Mandatory / Desired | Vendor response (Meets / Partially / Does not meet / Roadmap) | Comments.

## 4. Vendor Information Requested
- Company overview, years in business, ownership, financial stability
- Relevant experience and 3 references (ideally life sciences / temperature-controlled / regulated)
- Key personnel and account team
- Subcontractors and locations
- Quality certifications, audit history, insurance
- Security and data-handling practices (if systems or data are involved)
- Implementation / onboarding plan and timeline
- Risk, business-continuity and disaster-recovery approach

## 5. Pricing
Vendor-completed table: line item | unit | unit price | volume assumption | annual cost | one-time fees | assumptions. Require all fees (set-up, minimums, surcharges, escalators) to be disclosed.

## 6. Timeline
Issue date · intent-to-respond · question deadline · Hugel answers posted to all vendors · responses due · finalist presentations · decision · target start.

## 7. Evaluation Criteria
Weighted list totaling 100% (suggested starting point, adjust to the category): Capability and fit 30 · Quality/compliance 20 · Price/total cost 20 · Implementation and transition 10 · References and financial stability 10 · Service model and support 10.

## 8. Terms
Confidentiality, no obligation to award, response validity period, costs of responding borne by vendor, submission format and contact, single point of contact rule. Legal to review before issue.
